BUILT FOR THE DETAILS

Your point,
in working order.

Longitude first for GeoJSON and WKT. Latitude first where the target expects it. CoordSmith makes the difference visible before you copy.

  • 01

    Readable precision

    Monospaced coordinates and named representations help you catch mistakes.

  • 02

    A comfortable workspace

    Light and dark appearances, larger maps, and easily switchable Hungarian and English interfaces.

  • 03

    Maps when you want them

    Preview and pick points on an OpenStreetMap basemap, enabled by default. Expand maps to full screen and fit all geometry. Core tools work offline; tiles can be disabled in Settings.

LOCAL FIRST, BY DESIGN

Your coordinates stay in your workflow.

Core processing happens on your device. No account, ads, analytics SDKs, or custom telemetry. Favorites and history are stored locally.

Map tiles require network requests. Opening or sharing a point passes data to the app you choose. Your operating system may include local app data in its backups.

RELEASE STATUS

Made for iOS & Android.

CoordSmith is a release candidate for iOS 15 or later and Android 7.0 or later. It is not currently listed on the App Store or Google Play. Android builds can be installed directly by testers; iOS testing uses a simulator or a provisioned development device.

Test builds are supplied by the project owner. The application source repository is private; the website support tracker is public.
