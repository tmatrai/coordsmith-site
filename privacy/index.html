COORDSMITH / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

CoordSmith is a local-first geographic utility. This policy covers the app and this companion website.

What happens on your device

Coordinate parsing, conversion, calculations, and geometry inspection run locally. CoordSmith has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking service, or custom telemetry backend.

Favorites, input history, and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. History is capped at 50 deduplicated entries. You can clear it; disabling history also clears recorded entries. Large inputs over 100,000 characters are not added to history. Deleting the app generally removes its local data; Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled by the app configuration; iOS system backups may preserve or restore local app data according to your operating system settings. Local storage is not an encrypted vault.

Location is optional

CoordSmith requests location permission only when you choose to use your current location. The app uses that location to produce a point and show the accuracy supplied by the device. It does not run background location tracking. You can deny or revoke permission in system settings and continue using other app features.

Maps and other network activity

Online map tiles are initially disabled. If you enable them, viewing maps requests tiles from OpenStreetMap servers. Requests disclose your IP address, standard request information, and tile identifiers representing the area you are viewing. Map providers can infer that area even though CoordSmith does not send your pasted text or saved library to a custom backend. Maps may be unavailable offline.

OpenStreetMap requests are covered by the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. Opening a point in a map app or browser passes coordinates to that service. Sharing or copying a result puts that result in the destination you select or on the system clipboard, where platform rules govern access.

This website

The website has no analytics, ad pixels, embedded third-party maps, or externally hosted fonts. It stores only an appearance preference in your browser if you change the theme. Your hosting provider may process IP addresses and ordinary request logs to deliver and protect the site. GitHub Pages documents IP logging for security in its hosting documentation and privacy statement.

Support and control

If you choose to report an issue, the information you submit is processed by the support service. Avoid including sensitive coordinates, private locations, or personal information. No app diagnostics are automatically uploaded.

For privacy questions or a report, use the configured support channel. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
